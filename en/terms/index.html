1. The service

Glowbe is a bucket list app: you write down your dreams, place them on a globe, keep a memory of the ones you have achieved, and share them, if you wish, with your loved ones or the community.

Glowbe is free. If paid options appear one day, they will be optional and clearly presented before any payment; what is free today will not become paid without you being told.

2. Your account

  • Glowbe is for people aged 18 and over.
  • You give accurate information and keep your password to yourself. If you think someone is using your account, change your password and let us know.
  • One account per person, in your own name: no account in someone else's name.
  • You can delete your account at any time (see Delete my account).

3. Your content

Your dreams, photos and notes remain yours. So that Glowbe can display them, you allow us to host, reproduce and show them, only in the app and on the website, and only to the people you have chosen (“Only me”, “My loved ones”, “Everyone”). For an “Everyone” memory, this permission also covers the showcase on the app's home screen, without your first name. It ends when you delete the content or your account, except for what others have already taken with a “Me too!” (the dream's title, without your photo or note).

You only publish content you have the right to share. In particular:

  • you took your photos yourself, or you have their author's permission;
  • if other people can be recognised in a photo you make visible, you have their permission.

4. Community rules

Glowbe must stay a kind place. It is forbidden to post:

  • content that puts people in danger, including dangerous challenges;
  • personal information about other people without their consent;
  • advertising, spam or scams.

You can report content to us at bonjour@glowbe.fr (and soon directly in the app). We may remove content that breaks these rules, and suspend or delete its author's account, explaining why unless the law prevents it. You can challenge this decision by writing to us.

The challenges and ideas suggested by Glowbe are suggestions. You alone decide what you do: respect the law, your safety and that of others, and never put yourself in danger to achieve a dream.
